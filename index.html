About

A LITTLE ABOUT ME.

I spent the early part of my career in a radio newsroom, learning what makes a compelling story, who could best tell it, and how to bring it to life on deadline.

At CBS Radio, I scripted and produced The John Batchelor Show, a nationally syndicated program covering everything from Middle East geopolitics to global markets. The show’s co-host was Malcolm Hoenlein, a generational Jewish communal leader and diplomat. Working with journalists, diplomats, government officials, and policy experts gave me a firsthand understanding of how news is shaped and how international affairs unfold.

Some of my most interesting work, though, happened outside the booth. My work with Mr. Hoenlein extended to the Conference of Presidents of Major American Jewish Organizations, where I was involved in sensitive diplomatic and policy matters, from the Iran file and the Abraham Accords to energy policy and the repatriation of religious objects. Much of this work took place behind the scenes, teaching me the value of discretion, sound judgment, and staying composed in a crisis.

My career in public relations took me into a different set of challenges. I worked with women’s rights activists trapped in Afghanistan, led national media bookings for an agency reporter covering the Ukraine–Romania border, and supported novel initiatives ranging from Holocaust education at a Muslim school to projects with the Deputy Speaker of the New York City Council.

Today, I lead communications at Secure Community Network, the official safety and security organization for the Jewish community in North America. I oversee a team, manage national media relations, and advise the CEO and senior leadership, particularly during national crises such as the West Bloomfield terrorist attack. Beyond the daily news cycle, I write op-eds, develop executive messaging, and oversee integrated communications campaigns and collateral, including a Crisis Communications Guide now used by 145 Jewish Federations.

I am also an inaugural Media Fellow of the Foundation for Defense of Democracies’ National Security Network, alongside national security practitioners and journalists from leading media outlets.

What I enjoy most about this work is finding the story within a complex issue and learning how to tell it well. Sometimes that means getting a story onto the front page. Other times, it means helping an organization find the right words at a defining moment.
